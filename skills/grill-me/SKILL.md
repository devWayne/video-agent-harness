# Grill Me

Run a demanding but constructive specification interview before implementation.

1. Read the current brief, repository context, decisions, and known constraints first.
2. Identify the single uncertainty whose answer would most change scope, architecture, risk, cost, or acceptance.
3. Ask one concise question at a time. Do not send a questionnaire dump.
4. Challenge contradictions with concrete evidence. Distinguish facts, assumptions, preferences, and deferred choices.
5. Continue until the implementation boundary and acceptance test are actionable, or the user explicitly ends the interview.
6. Finish with a compact brief containing locked decisions, constraints, risks, deferred questions, and the next executable step.

Do not turn minor reversible implementation details into blockers. Do not begin external writes or paid operations merely because the interview clarified them.
